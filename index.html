Quizzler Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Quizzler is a private automation tool that creates short "Would You Rather" quiz videos and uploads them to the Quizzler YouTube channel (@The-Quizzler-101). It is operated by the channel owner for their own channel only and is not offered to the public.

Information we collect

Quizzler does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data. It has no user accounts, sign-up forms, cookies, analytics or tracking.

Google / YouTube access

Quizzler uses Google OAuth to access only the channel owner's own YouTube account, with a single permission:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload – used solely to upload videos to the owner's channel automatically.

Quizzler does not read, modify or delete any other YouTube or Google account data, and it does not access the accounts of viewers or any other users.

Storage of credentials

The OAuth token used for uploading is stored only on the channel owner's own computer. It is never transmitted to any third party other than Google.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Quizzler's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account permissions page.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the channel owner via the Quizzler YouTube channel.
